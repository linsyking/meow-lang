\paragraph{Flat lazy passes.}
Proposition~\ref{prop:lpcons} located the call-free lazy-pass machine's
extra definedness: every point of it comes from a discarded replacement.
It did not say whether that definedness is \emph{realizable} -- whether
the lazy-pass denotation of a call-free expression is the partial
function of \emph{some} plain expression.  The answer is yes, by an
explicit translation, and so the call-free fragment of the lazy-pass
runtime denotes exactly $\mathrm{L}$: without self-reference, the
operator's non-strictness buys definedness, not power.

Two small constructions carry the translation.  First, the
\emph{coercion}: fixing $\sigma \in \Sigma$,
\begin{align*}
  \gamma(X) \;\triangleq\; \mathtt{if}\big(\mathtt{eq}(X,\epsilon),\;
  \sigma,\; X\big),
\end{align*}
total, with $\gamma(w) = w$ for $w \neq \epsilon$ and
$\gamma(\epsilon) = \sigma$ -- a pattern wrapped in $\gamma$ is never
empty.  Second, the \emph{occurrence test} for total values: for total
$U, V$ over $\{\sigma_1, \sigma_2\}$,
\begin{align*}
  \mathtt{occ}(U,V) \;\triangleq\;
  \mathtt{if}\Big(\mathtt{eq}(U,\sigma_2),\;
    \mathtt{if}\big(\mathtt{eq}([\,\sigma_2\sigma_2/\sigma_2\,]V,\,V),\,
      \bot,\,\top\big),\;
    \mathtt{if}\big(\mathtt{eq}([\,\sigma_2/\gamma(U)\,]V,\,V),\,
      \bot,\,\top\big)\Big),
\end{align*}
total, with value $\top$ iff $U$'s value occurs in $V$'s whenever
$U \neq \epsilon$ (the value at $U = \epsilon$ is never read).  Soundness
is the observation that a pass whose replacement differs from its
pattern changes its scrutinee iff the pattern occurs -- the replacement
$\sigma_2$ differs from the pattern on the second branch by the case
split, and the first branch's $[\,\sigma_2\sigma_2/\sigma_2\,]$ strictly
grows on firing -- so the $\mathtt{eq}$-comparison detects the firing.
No freshness assumption is needed: the only degeneracy, pattern $=
$ replacement, is routed around by the test on $U$ itself.
(Verified: $225$ value pairs, $|u|, |v| \leq 3$, zero undefined, zero
wrong answers.)

\begin{theorem}
    \label{thm:flatlazy}
    (\emph{Flat lazy passes denote $\mathrm{L}$.})  For every call-free
    expression $E$ over any alphabet with $|\Sigma| \geq 2$ there is a
    call-free expression $E^{+}$ of the plain calculus with
    $\llbracket E^{+} \rrbracket \;=\; \llbracket E \rrbracket^{\mathrm{lp}}$
    -- the same partial function, domain included -- and $E^{+}$ is
    polynomially larger than $E$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    Two mutually recursive translations, by structural induction on $E$.
    The \emph{value part} $\Phi(E)$ replaces every pattern by its
    coerced version:
    $\Phi(X_i) = X_i$, $\Phi(w) = w$,
    $\Phi(E \cdot G) = \Phi(E) \cdot \Phi(G)$, and
    \begin{align*}
      \Phi\big(\,[\,R/P\,]\,T\,\big) \;=\;
      \big[\,\Phi R\,/\,\gamma(\Phi P)\,\big]\,\Phi T .
    \end{align*}
    Every pattern in $\Phi(E)$ is a $\gamma$-image, so $\Phi(E)$ is
    total.  On an input where the lazy-pass machine defines $E$, the
    forced patterns are nonempty (the machine forces them), so each
    $\gamma$ acts as the identity there; an inert pass contributes its
    scrutinee either way; and a firing pass forces $R$, where $\Phi R$
    agrees with $R$ by induction.  Hence $\Phi(E)$ is total and agrees
    with $\llbracket E \rrbracket^{\mathrm{lp}}$ on its domain.

    The \emph{domain part} $F(E) \in \{\top, \bot\}$, total, indicates
    the domain: $F(X_i) = F(w) = \top$;
    $F(E \cdot G) = F(E) \wedge F(G)$; and
    \begin{align*}
      F\big(\,[\,R/P\,]\,T\,\big) \;=\;
      F(T) \,\wedge\, F(P) \,\wedge\, \mathtt{isne}(\Phi P)
      \,\wedge\, \big(\neg\,\mathtt{occ}(\Phi P, \Phi T)
      \;\vee\; F(R)\big),
    \end{align*}
    where $\mathtt{isne}(X) \triangleq
    \mathtt{if}(\mathtt{eq}(X,\epsilon),\bot,\top)$; note the
    \emph{conjunction}: an empty forced pattern must falsify $F$
    outright, not vacuously satisfy it.  The boolean connectives are
    $\mathtt{if}$/$\mathtt{eq}$/$\cdot$ combinations of the values, all
    total.  All sub-expressions of $F$ are total -- the partial
    scrutinee and pattern of the node enter only through $F$ itself,
    and their \emph{values} only through the total $\Phi$ images -- so
    the recursion is well-defined and $F(E) = \top$ exactly on
    $\mathrm{dom}(\llbracket E \rrbracket^{\mathrm{lp}})$ by the
    forcing discipline.

    Finally the \emph{guard}: $E^{+} \triangleq
    [\,\sigma_1\,/\,\mathtt{if}(F(E),\sigma_1,\epsilon)\,]\;\Phi(E)$.
    On the domain the pattern is $\sigma_1$ and the pass is
    $[\,\sigma_1/\sigma_1\,]$, the identity; off the domain the pattern
    is empty and the expression is undefined.  The size is polynomial:
    each level of the recursion multiplies by a constant (the toolkit
    constructions behind $\mathtt{if}$, $\mathtt{eq}$, $\cdot$ are
    fixed), so $|E^{+}| = |E|^{O(1)}$.

    (Verified exhaustively on the depth-$\leq 2$ call-free space over
    $\{a,b\}$ -- $599{,}844$ expressions -- where the lazy-pass machine
    has $193$ denotations that no depth-$2$ eager expression has: for
    the simplest witness of each, $E^{+}$ reproduces the lazy partial
    function on all $31$ inputs $|S| \leq 4$ with zero mismatches;
    likewise for $250$ random depth-$2$ and $120$ random depth-$3$
    expressions ($|E^{+}|$ up to ${\sim}3.1 \times 10^4$ nodes); the
    translation was additionally cross-checked against the reference
    machine of this section on $31$ expressions, and $25$ of the
    witnesses were re-verified on all $127$ inputs $|S| \leq 6$; zero
    mismatches throughout, and $\Phi$ total throughout.)
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:flatlazy} completes Proposition~\ref{prop:lpcons} and
sharpens the border this section draws.  Eager evaluation and lazy
arguments leave the reachable class unchanged even with recursion
(Theorem~\ref{thm:lazyargs}); lazy passes make recursion universal
through the gate.  Theorem~\ref{thm:flatlazy} shows that the evaluation
order \emph{alone} -- the operator's own non-strictness, without a
self-reference to guard -- never leaves $\mathrm{L}$: the gate needs
the loop.  In the terms of Proposition~\ref{prop:lpcons}, every
discarded replacement is not merely harmless but \emph{recoverable}:
the definedness it would have destroyed is exactly the definedness the
guard re-inserts.
